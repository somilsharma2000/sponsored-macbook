The Experiment
What is The Sponsored MacBook?

It's a 30-day public marketing experiment. One MacBook. Twenty sponsor positions on the lid. Every position tracked with QR codes. Every interaction measured. Every milestone shared publicly.

The campaign ends with a live MacBook purchase and unboxing — followed by a 12-month case study documenting the journey, the data, and the results.

If the campaign doesn't reach ₹6L in 30 days, every sponsor gets a full refund. The failure becomes part of the case study.

The Problem

₹6L for a MacBook Pro 16" M5 Max. ₹0 in budget. Traditional options: save for months, take a loan, or give up.

The Experiment

Sell the MacBook as advertising real estate. 20 positions. 4 tiers. Real brands. Real money. Real tracking. Public story.

03
Why This Is Interesting
Why would a brand participate?

Because "we sponsored a MacBook" is a more interesting story than "we ran another Meta ad."

  • It's a PR story. A marketing experiment that generates attention, content, and conversation.
  • It's a content asset. Every sponsor gets social content featuring their brand on the MacBook.
  • It's a case study. Every sponsor is featured in a published 12-month marketing case study.
  • It's category exclusivity. One brand per category. If you claim "audio," no competitor joins.
  • It's a public bet. The campaign succeeds or fails in public — either way, it's a story.
  • It costs less than one week of Meta ads. ₹10K–₹1L for a permanent asset vs. ₹10K for a week of ads with no lasting value.

Tracking & Analytics
Measurable. Transparent. Real.

Every sponsor gets a unique QR code and unique tracking URL. All interactions are measured:

QR Scans

Every time someone scans your QR code on the MacBook. Tracked in real-time.

Profile Visits

Every visit to your dedicated sponsor page on the campaign website.

Click-throughs

Every outbound click from your profile to your website.

Tour Exposure

Documentation of every event/location where the MacBook (and your logo) appeared.

We don't promise ROI. We promise independently measurable campaign exposure data. The value is the story, the content, and the data — not guaranteed impressions.
