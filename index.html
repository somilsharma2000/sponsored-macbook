The Experiment

What is this?

This isn't a giveaway. This isn't crowdfunding. This is a public marketing experiment.

The premise: I want a MacBook Pro. I have ₹0 to spend. So I'm running a 30-day public experiment: can 20 brands fund it entirely through sponsorship?

20 positions. 1 Crown (₹1L). 4 Founding (₹50K). 10 Standard (₹25K). 5 Community (₹10K). Each position gets physical space on the MacBook lid, a unique QR code, a dedicated landing page, and full tracking.

The public bet: If I don't hit ₹6L in 30 days, every sponsor gets a full refund AND I publish a detailed case study of why it failed. The failure is part of the story.

Why would a brand pay? This isn't just a sticker — it's a PR story, a content asset, a case study, and a social media moment. "We sponsored a MacBook" is more interesting than "we ran another Meta ad." And at ₹10K-₹50K, it costs less than one week of Meta ads.

What every sponsor gets: Physical brand placement, unique QR code with live tracking, dedicated profile page, social content, and inclusion in a published 12-month case study.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Every objection addressed honestly. No spin.

Why should I sponsor a MacBook? ▼
You're not buying a sticker — you're buying into a public marketing experiment that generates PR, content, and social attention. "We sponsored a MacBook" is a story people share. It costs less than one week of Meta ads. You get tracked QR traffic, a dedicated profile, social content, and a 12-month case study with your brand in it. The real value is the story, not the sticker.
How many people will see this? ▼
We don't guarantee views — that would be dishonest. What we guarantee: physical placement on a MacBook that travels to co-working spaces, tech meetups, and cafés. A unique QR code tracked in real-time. Social content featuring your brand. A public campaign page. The viral potential is real but not guaranteed. What IS guaranteed is the infrastructure, the story, and the case study.
What happens if you don't raise enough? ▼
100% refund guarantee. If we don't hit ₹6L in 30 days, every sponsor gets a full refund — no questions asked. Alternatively, you can roll forward: your position is locked in for Campaign #2 at the same price. We're transparent about progress at all times. No money is spent until the MacBook is purchased.
Is this legal / a scam? ▼
This is a legitimate commercial sponsorship. We issue GST invoices. Payments are processed via Razorpay (RBI-authorized payment aggregator). We use a registered business entity. Sponsor agreements are available for review. We comply with ASCI guidelines. Every transaction is publicly tracked. Full refund guarantee.
What's the ROI? ▼
We don't promise ROI — that would be dishonest. What we deliver: tracked QR scans, tracked landing page visitors, tracked outbound clicks, social content with your brand, a public case study, and PR value of being part of a novel experiment. For ₹10K-₹50K, the question is: what else could this money buy? A week of Meta ads with no lasting asset. This is different — it's a story.
Can I choose my position? ▼
Yes — first come, first served. The Crown (₹1L) is the center of the lid. Founding (₹50K) surround the Crown. Standard (₹25K) fill the outer lid. Community (₹10K) are smaller spots for startups.
How does payment work? ▼
After you apply, we schedule a 15-minute call. Then we send a formal invoice via Razorpay — pay via UPI, cards, or net banking. You receive a GST invoice. No money is collected informally.
Why should I be the first sponsor? ▼
The first 3 sponsors get permanent Founding status — a badge on all campaign materials, bonus social content (2x posts), priority in the case study, and first right of refusal for Campaign #2. Being first is a risk, but it comes with permanent rewards.
